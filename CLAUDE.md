# My Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business. Use it to draft copy, build sequences, and organise campaign assets — not to send anything to real people.

## Folders

- **brand/** — brand voice, tone guidelines, and reference copy
- **leads/** — audience segments and lead notes
- **campaigns/** — email drafts, sequences, and campaign plans
- **notes/** — research, skill notes, and miscellaneous references

@brand/voice.md

## Rules

- Write in plain English. No jargon, no buzzwords.
- Never send email to real people, and never generate real email addresses to send to.
- Ask before deleting any file or folder.
