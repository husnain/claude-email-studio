# Customer Reviews — Batch 3 (41–60)

---

**Review 41** ★★★★★
**Rehan Sohail — Owner, Al-Madina Grill, Oldham**

I have one member of staff who's been resistant to any technology change since 2015. Within two services she was comfortable with MezbanPOS. I think it's the layout — the most common actions are always visible. You don't have to dig through sub-menus to split a bill or apply a discount. That made her comfortable, and if it's intuitive enough for her, it's intuitive enough for anyone.

**Review 42** ★★★★☆
**Sidra Nawaz — Owner, Karahi King**

The table reservations view gives us a colour-coded floor plan — green for occupied, amber for dessert stage, red for tables that have been seated more than 90 minutes. That last one is new to us and it changed how the front of house manages turnover. We can gently prompt long-seated tables without looking like we're rushing them. One feature request: let me change the 90-minute threshold.

**Ghazala Ikram — Owner, Desi Comfort Kitchen**

**Review 45** ★★★★☆
**Kamran Zafar — Co-owner, Raan & Rice House**

We do a large Sunday buffet in addition to à la carte. I set up buffet pricing as a separate cover-based item and it works. The slight friction: when a table switches from buffet to à la carte mid-service (some people start with buffet then order extra dishes), the order has to be split manually across two categories. The system supports it but the steps aren't obvious. Took us a few tries to work it out.

**Review 46** ★★★☆☆
**Rukhsana Ahmed — Owner, Bake & Biryani**

The system works fine for savoury items. I have a baked goods section that changes daily. Adding daily specials is fast — I can add an item from the iPad in under a minute. The problem is I can't set an item to auto-expire at end of day. I have to manually remove it each evening or it stays on the menu. With fifteen daily bakes to remove every night that's a lot of tapping.
