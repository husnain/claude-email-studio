# Customer Reviews — Batch 2 (21–40)

---

**Review 21** ★★★★☆
**Tariq Bhatti — Owner, Namkeen Karahi, Bradford**

We handle about 200 covers on a weekend. The system held up fine. Where I noticed the most difference is table handover — when a server's shift ends mid-service, they hand the table to the next person in two taps and all the order history is right there. No more scribbled notes passed between staff. Lost one order in three months and I'm honestly not sure MezbanPOS was the cause.

---

**Review 22** ★★☆☆☆
**Mariam Yousuf — Manager, The Korma Garden**

The system works but the support hours don't. We run dinner service until midnight. If something breaks at 10pm we're on our own until the next morning. There's a support email but no live chat outside business hours. For a restaurant, business hours are exactly when things go wrong. I'd pay extra for 24/7 support if it existed.

---

**Review 23** ★★★★★
**Sajid Akhtar — Owner, Pind Punjabi Kitchen**

The offline mode is the feature I didn't know I needed. Our broadband went down for 40 minutes during a Sunday lunch. The system kept taking orders and payments locally, then synced when we came back online. We told zero customers. That would have been a disaster on our old system.

---

**Review 24** ★★★★☆
**Lubna Chaudhary — Owner, Dhaba Delights**

Good system overall. The stock tracking is basic — it tracks items sold but doesn't warn me when I'm running low on specific ingredients. For a restaurant doing prep in the morning that's a real gap. I track stock manually in a separate spreadsheet, which feels backwards. Would love ingredient-level inventory, even a simple version.

---

**Review 25** ★★★☆☆
**Nadeem Butt — Owner, Royal Tikka House**

Decent enough for the price. My specific complaint: the discount feature only does percentage discounts. We sometimes give a fixed £5 voucher to regulars and right now I'd have to calculate the percentage manually or add a negative-price item to the order. Neither is elegant. I told support and they said fixed-amount discounts are on the list. I'll take their word for it.

---

**Review 26** ★★★★★
**Rubina Iqbal — Owner, Mela Sweet & Savoury**

We sell sweets by weight and serve hot food to tables. I didn't think one POS could handle both. MezbanPOS has a weight-based item type — the server types in the number of grams and it calculates the price. We were doing this with a separate till for the sweets counter. Now it's one system and one end-of-day report.

---

**Review 27** ★★★★☆
**Faisal Nazir — Co-owner, Green Chilli Grill**

The table map editor is genuinely good. I reshaped our floor layout during refurbishment and updated the map in about fifteen minutes — dragging and dropping table shapes, changing covers, labelling sections. No one had to come on-site. The only improvement I'd suggest: an option to lock the map so a server can't accidentally drag a table during service.

---

**Review 28** ★★☆☆☆
**Tahira Baig — Manager, Zafrani Biryani Bar**

We onboarded in two days. The issue showed up later: at the end of month one, the VAT summary report had a formatting error that made it look like our VAT liability was zero. It wasn't. I spotted it because the number looked wrong. Support acknowledged the bug and fixed it quickly, but I shouldn't have to audit my own tax reports for arithmetic errors. That shook my confidence.

---

**Review 29** ★★★★★
**Hamid Qazi — Owner, Lahori Paye House**

We're a small place — twelve tables, two servers, two in the kitchen. I didn't need a complicated system. MezbanPOS's simple mode is exactly that: add items, take payment, done. The more advanced features I'll never use are there if we grow. It doesn't feel like I'm paying for things I'll never touch.

**Review 30** ★★★★☆
**Zunaira Ali — Owner, Nihari Stop, Glasgow**

One specific thing I want to mention: the multi-language receipt option. We have customers who prefer Urdu on their receipt. I turned it on in settings and it worked on the first try. Some of our older regulars commented on it. That's a thoughtful detail that none of the other systems I looked at offered.

The system works fine on iPad once you know what to do, but new users will hit that wall.

**Review 32** ★★★★★

Our busiest night is Thursday. We do around 130 covers and by 8pm every table is occupied. I watched my head server run the floor for a full service with zero paper — she took orders on the handheld, they went straight to the kitchen screen, and payment happened at the table. **Review 35** ★★★★★
**Shahid Cheema — Owner, Murgh Cholay Palace**

The order bumping on the kitchen display is exactly how I wanted it to work. When a dish is ready, the chef taps it off. The server gets a notification on their handheld. The customer is never waiting with an empty table while the food gets cold at the pass. We shaved about four minutes off average service time in the first month.

---

**Review 36** ★★★★☆
**Bushra Mian — Owner, Chai & Rusk Café**

I run a café with a loyal morning crowd and a quieter afternoon. I set up two price tiers — normal and happy-hour — and scheduled them to switch automatically at 3pm. It works perfectly. The only quirk: if I make a price change manually during happy hour, the auto-schedule still kicks in correctly at the next changeover, but it doesn't tell you that it's about to override your manual change. I found that out the hard way.

**Review 37** ★★☆☆☆
**Jawad Tariq — Owner, Smoky Nihari Co.**

Three issues I've logged in four months: menu images not loading on terminals after a large image upload, receipt printer going offline silently, and the split-payment screen freezing once during a group booking. Each was fixed but I shouldn't have three separate bugs in my first four months. I'll give it another few months before I make a recommendation to other owners.

**Review 38** ★★★★★
**Anam Farooq — Owner, The Desi Pantry**

I was nervous about the transition because we have a lot of set menus with complex combos. I spent an afternoon entering everything and it handled nested options well — a set menu item that has a choice of starter, main, and dessert, all within one press. My old system made me create each combo as a separate product. This is cleaner and my menu board matches what's in the POS now.

---

**Review 39** ★★★★☆
**Muzammil Hussain — Manager, Karachi Nights Restaurant**

The void and refund process is one of the best things about the system. The old system we used required a manager override and a printed log for every void. MezbanPOS still requires a manager PIN (good) but the void is instant, the digital log is automatic, and I can review all voids by date at the end of the week. No mystery voids.
