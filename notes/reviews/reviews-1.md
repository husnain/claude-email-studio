# Customer Reviews — Batch 1 (1–20)

---

**Review 1** ★★★★★
**Tariq Hussain — Owner, Spice Route Karachi**

We've been running on MezbanPOS for six weeks. Split billing used to take my staff four minutes per table on a busy night — now it's under a minute. The table layout screen is exactly how our floor looks, which sounds small but it means new servers stop confusing table 7 and table 17. No complaints yet that actually stuck.

---

**Review 2** ★★★☆☆
**Amina Siddiqui — Manager, The Lahori Kitchen, Manchester**

Setup was straightforward. The onboarding guide walked us through menu entry in about an hour. What I didn't expect: the daily sales report emails don't include a breakdown by category, only total revenue. I need to know if our breakfast trade is growing or not. Support said a category filter is "coming soon," which is not an answer I can give my owner.

---

**Review 3** ★★★★☆
**Farhan Malik — Owner, Chaikhana Bites**

The kitchen display screen replaced our paper ticket system on day one. Chefs can mark items ready individually rather than the whole ticket, so a table with two starters and three mains doesn't stall because one main is slow. Lost maybe one order in the first week while staff adjusted. Solid system.

---

**Review 4** ★★☆☆☆
**Nadia Chaudhry — Floor Manager, Roti Republic**

We signed up during the beta. The app crashed twice during Friday dinner service in week two — once during a card payment, which left us manually entering the amount on the terminal. The crash logs didn't tell us anything useful. Support was responsive but it took three days to confirm it was a known bug. That's too slow when you're in the middle of a service.

---

**Review 5** ★★★★★
**Bilal Qureshi — Owner, Desi Flame, Birmingham**

I switched from a legacy system that charged me per terminal. MezbanPOS charges per location, so I added two more handhelds for the patio at no extra cost. That alone paid for the subscription in the first month. The menu sync between all terminals happens fast — under ten seconds when I add a daily special.

---

**Review 6** ★★★★☆
**Raheela Anwar — Owner, The Nihari House**

The staff PIN login is a good idea. Each server can see their own tips and table history without getting into anyone else's orders. One issue: if a server forgets their PIN mid-service there's no quick reset — the manager has to log into the admin panel from a separate device. During a rush that's about three minutes of faff. Would be better as a table-side override.

---

**Review 7** ★★★★★
**Kamran Javed — Owner, Zaiqa Fine Dine**

We did a full refurbishment and switched POS systems at the same time. I was expecting chaos. The menu import from our old system's CSV worked on the second attempt — first attempt had a character encoding issue in some Urdu dish names. Support fixed it in a couple of hours. By night two we were running normally.

---

**Review 8** ★★★☆☆
**Saima Tariq — Manager, Haleem & Co**

The reservation module shows walk-ins and booked covers on the same screen, which is exactly what we needed for our 40-cover dining room. The problem is it doesn't connect to our Google Business page, so staff still have to manually check two places for bookings. I was told integration was on the roadmap but I've heard that phrase a lot.

---

**Review 9** ★★★★★
**Imran Sheikh — Owner, Mango Tree Restaurant**

Five-star. The end-of-day cash reconciliation used to take my manager 25 minutes with a calculator and a bit of hope. MezbanPOS gives her a printed expected-cash figure and she checks against the drawer. Takes six minutes now. She said it's the first time in three years she's not dreaded closing.

---

**Review 10** ★★★★☆
**Zara Khan — Owner, Karahi Palace**

We cater private events about once a month. I built a separate menu for event pricing and switching between the restaurant menu and the event menu is two taps. Only downside: the event menu doesn't save per-event — if I want different pricing for two events on the same weekend, I'd have to manually update it each time. For us that's rare enough to live with.

---

**Review 11** ★★★☆☆
**Omar Farooq — Co-owner, The Butter Chicken Club**

The day-to-day works fine. My issue is the reporting. I want to see which servers are upselling effectively — which ones are selling more drinks per cover, for example. Right now I'd have to export the data and do it myself in a spreadsheet. For a system at this price point, that analysis should be built in.

**Review 12** ★★★★★
**Sana Baig — Owner, Qorma & Chai, Edinburgh**

We opened three months ago and I'd never run a restaurant before. MezbanPOS was the one part of opening that actually worked as described. The demo I watched online matched what I got. The menu builder is forgiving — I changed our prices four times in the first fortnight and it took thirty seconds each time. No data entry disasters.

**Ahsan Riaz — Owner, Kebabish Corner**

**Review 14** ★★★★★
**Hina Aziz — Manager, Dumpukht Dining**

We have a takeaway counter and a sit-down section. MezbanPOS lets us run them as separate order flows on the same account. The takeaway queue is on a screen behind the counter and the dine-in tickets go to the kitchen display. Staff stopped mixing up orders within the first week. The workflow made sense immediately.

---

**Review 15** ★★★★☆
**Muneeb Rashid — Owner, Lahori Chargha House**

Modifier setup is flexible. We have a lot of "extra sauce," "no onion," "half portion" variations and I entered about 60 modifiers without hitting any limit. The only thing I'd improve: when a modifier makes a dish more expensive, the updated price doesn't show on the customer receipt unless I enable a setting buried in the print options. Took me a week to notice customers were seeing old prices on paper.

---

**Review 16** ★★★☆☆
**Fatima Nadeem — Owner, Peshwari Flavours**

Good for small menus. We have 140 items across lunch and dinner and the menu grid starts to feel cluttered on a 10-inch screen. I've heard they're redesigning the menu view, but right now servers slow down when scanning for a specific item. We reorganised into fewer categories, which helped, but I shouldn't have to compromise my menu structure to fit the software.

---

**Review 17** ★★★★★
**Ali Hassan — Owner, Murgh Makhani Modern**

The loyalty programme module is the reason I stayed. I tried three other POS systems that all said they had loyalty. MezbanPOS's version actually stamps a customer's record at the point of payment without any extra steps from the server. After two months we had 180 customers enrolled and I didn't have to push anyone to sign up.

---

**Review 18** ★★★★☆
**Rida Hussain — Manager, Chai Stop Café**

Mostly positive. The system is fast and the touchscreen is responsive even when hands are wet or floury, which matters in a café kitchen. The one thing that caught us out: the auto-print for kitchen tickets defaults to printing every single modifier on its own line, so a customised order comes out as a 15-line ticket. The print layout settings need a compact mode and right now it doesn't have one.

---

**Review 19** ★★★☆☆
**Khalid Memon — Owner, Sindhi Thali House**

The price is fair and the basics work. I'm giving three stars because I spent two hours on a Sunday trying to set up a staff rota integration with an external app and found out MezbanPOS doesn't have an API yet. That's a significant gap if you're running more than one location. I'm on a trial and I'll be watching whether this gets built before my renewal decision.

---

**Review 20** ★★★★★
**Ayesha Chughtai — Owner, Biryani & Beyond, Leeds**

Switched mid-service on a quieter Tuesday. It was not as scary as I expected. The import of our item catalogue took about 20 minutes including photos. By the Friday rush the entire team was comfortable with it. The old system we used made me dread every software update. MezbanPOS updates happen in the background — we haven't once arrived to a broken screen.
