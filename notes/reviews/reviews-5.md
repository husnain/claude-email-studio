**Review 81** ★★★★★
**Haris Chughtai — Owner, Murgh Handi Central**

The system handles our Sunday brunch service, which is completely different from our weekday dinner setup — different menu, different pricing, different section layout. I created a "Brunch" mode that swaps everything: the menu, the table map, and even the kitchen printer routing. Switching between modes takes three taps. I did not expect that level of flexibility at this price.

**Zulaikha Farooq — Manager, Raan & Rotis**
