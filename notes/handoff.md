# Handoff Note

## What each group cares about most
- **Managers**: keeping service moving; zero tech friction on shift
- **POS Explorers**: been burned before; need proof there's no catch (no contracts, no hidden fees)
- **Restaurant Owners**: business cost — a bad POS is a money problem; early pricing is a financial win

## The two angles
- **Angle A (Calm Daily Ritual)**: frames MezbanPOS as the quiet tool that just works — open, run, close
- **Angle B (Discovering the Right POS)**: frames it as the end of a search — you've been looking, here it is

## Pick: Angle A

Angle B assumes the reader is mid-search and self-aware about it. Managers aren't evaluating POS systems — they're on shift. Restaurant Owners aren't browsing; they already know the problem. Angle A meets them where they are: tired of friction, not hunting for a category winner. It also lands better for Managers specifically, who care about their daily rhythm more than any discovery narrative. Reserve Angle B framing for POS Explorers only in later emails.
