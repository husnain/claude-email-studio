# Email 03: Early Pricing Closes 31 December

**Send date:** Wednesday 23 December 2026  
**Timing:** Day 16 — nine days before the pricing deadline  
**Segment:** All waitlist contacts who have not yet subscribed  
**Goal:** Create genuine urgency around the 31 December pricing deadline and address the "what's the catch" objection directly.

---

## Subject Lines

1. Early pricing closes 31 Dec *(recommended — specific, honest, no manufactured hype)*
2. Lock this in before the new year
3. 8 days left at the launch price

## Preview Text

After 31 December, the waitlist price is gone. Here's what it includes.

## Body

Hi [First Name],

Quick note: the early pricing for waitlist members closes on **31 December**.

Here's what the launch pricing includes:

- Waitlist-only rate, locked in for 12 months
- Free migration from your current POS system

If MezbanPOS isn't working for your restaurant after a few weeks, you can walk away with no penalty and no awkward cancellation call. We built it this way because we're confident it'll work — and because we don't think locking people into contracts is a good way to run a business.

You have nine days. The deadline is real.
