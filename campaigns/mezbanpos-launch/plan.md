# MezbanPOS Launch Campaign Plan

**Campaign period:** 7 December – 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)
**Goal:** 300 waitlist members start a paid subscription by 3 January 2027
**Channel:** Email only
**List size:** ~2,000 contacts across three segments

---

## 1. Campaign Overview

**Campaign name:** "You're first in line"

**One-sentence summary:** A four-week email sequence that converts 15% of the MezbanPOS waitlist into paying subscribers by giving each of the three audience segments a reason that fits where they already are.

**Primary objective:** 300 paid subscriptions from ~2,000 waitlist contacts by end of Week 4 (15% conversion rate).

**Secondary objectives:**
- Re-engage lapsed waitlist contacts before the launch window closes
- Establish MezbanPOS as the practical choice for restaurant owners tired of clunky POS systems
- Collect early testimonials from Daily Users to use in Weeks 3–4

---

## 2. Target Audience

The waitlist has three segments. Each needs a different entry point.

### Curious (~800 people, ~40%)
Signed up after hearing about MezbanPOS but have shown little follow-up engagement. They know the name; they do not know why it matters to their restaurant. Pain point: they are aware something could be better about how they run their floor, but they have not committed to doing anything about it.

**Goal with this group:** make the problem concrete and show MezbanPOS solves it simply. Convert ~8% (64 subscriptions).

### Explorer (~700 people, ~35%)
Looked more closely — opened previous emails, visited the product page, or replied to a waitlist survey. They are evaluating options and want to know if MezbanPOS is worth switching to. Pain point: they have tried one or two POS tools before and been burned by complexity, hidden fees, or poor support.

**Goal with this group:** address their comparison anxiety and give them one clear reason to choose MezbanPOS now. Convert ~17% (119 subscriptions).

### Daily Users (~500 people, ~25%)
Beta testers, demo participants, or frequent engagers who have already seen the product work. They are the warmest group. Pain point: they want to get started but may be waiting for the right moment, or for a small reassurance about pricing or onboarding.

**Goal with this group:** remove the last friction point and close. Convert ~23% (117 subscriptions).

**Combined target: 300 subscriptions.**

---

## 3. Key Messages

**Core message:** MezbanPOS is built for the way restaurants actually work — not how software companies think they do.

### Supporting messages by segment

**Curious**
- "Running a restaurant is complicated. Your POS system does not have to be."
- Proof point: setup time is under 30 minutes, no IT help needed.

**Explorer**
- "You have been burned before. Here is what we did differently."
- Proof point: no annual contract, cancel any time, free migration from your current system.

**Daily Users**
- "You have already seen it work. Here is how to make it yours."
- Proof point: early-subscriber pricing locked for 12 months; onboarding call included.

### Shared across all segments
- Waitlist members get access before the general public
- Early pricing is only available during the launch window
- Real human support — not just a help centre

---

## 4. Channel Strategy

**Email is the only channel.** The entire list came through the waitlist form, so email is both the natural and the highest-trust channel.

**Why email works here:**
- The audience opted in specifically to hear about this launch — open rates should be above average
- Segmentation lets us write directly to where each group sits, reducing unsubscribes
- No spend required; effort is in copy quality and timing

**Format guidance:**
- Plain text or minimal HTML — restaurant owners are busy; dense templates get deleted
- Subject lines under 50 characters, front-loaded with the clearest benefit or hook
- One CTA per email, always linking to the subscription or signup page
- Mobile-first — most will read on their phone between shifts

## 7. Success Metrics

**Primary KPI:** 300 paid subscriptions by 3 January 2027

| Metric | Target | How to track |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| Paid subscriptions | 300 | Subscription platform dashboard |
| Email open rate | ≥35% (waitlist lists tend to run warm) | Email platform |
| Email click-through rate | ≥8% | Email platform |
| Unsubscribe rate | <1% per send | Email platform |
| Conversion by segment | Curious 8%, Explorer 17%, Daily Users 23% | Subscription platform, tagged by segment |
| Revenue from campaign | Depends on pricing tier | Subscription platform |

**Reporting cadence:** Check open and click rates 24 hours after each send. Review subscription numbers every Monday. Final campaign report on 5 January 2027.

**What to watch for:**
- If open rates drop below 25% by Week 2, the subject line direction needs to change
- If Curious segment CTR stays below 3% after Week 1, simplify the ask — offer a demo call before pushing the subscription CTA
- If Daily Users are not converting by Week 2, dig into pricing or onboarding friction

## 9. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Low open rates from lapsed Curious subscribers | Medium | Send a re-permissioning email in Week 1 for anyone who has not opened in 6+ months; clean the list before launch |

| Pricing page not live before launch | Low | Add this as a hard dependency and confirm with the team by 3 Dec; delay launch by one week if not ready rather than send without a destination |
