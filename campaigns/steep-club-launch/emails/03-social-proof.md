# Email 03 — Social Proof

**Send date:** Mon 2026-12-14
**Segment:** All waitlist members (prioritise non-converters)
**Status:** Draft

---

## Subject line options

1. What the first members are saying
2. One week in — here's what people found
3. Early members: here's what they said

*Recommended: option 1. Honest framing, low-pressure, curiosity-driven.*

## Preview text

Real feedback from people who joined last week.

---

## Purpose

Build confidence for people who opened the launch email but haven't subscribed yet. Use real or representative member quotes to show the product is working. Removes "is this actually good?" as a reason not to join.

---

## Timing

Send Monday 2026-12-14, 8–9 am. One full week after the launch email.

If engagement on the launch email was strong (open rate ≥ 40%), send this as written. If open rate was below 20%, consider leading with a re-send of the launch email to non-openers first (Friday 2026-12-11) before sending this.
