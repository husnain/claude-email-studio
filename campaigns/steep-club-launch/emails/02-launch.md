Hi [First name],

The Steep Club is open.

Here's how it works.

Every month, we put together a box of four to six teas you won't find in a supermarket. Each one is sourced directly — we know the farm, the harvest, and the people who picked it. Every tea comes with a short note telling you what it is, where it's from, and how to brew it.

Before your first box ships, you answer a 90-second quiz. It tells us what you like and what you don't. We use that to shape what lands at your door.

If something in a box isn't right for you, tell us. We'll fix the next one.

**[Join now — £[X]/month]**

No long commitment. Cancel or pause any time.

If you have questions, just reply to this email. We read them.

Talk soon,

[Name]
The Steep Club
