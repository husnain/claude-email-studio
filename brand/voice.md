# Brand Voice Guide

## Our voice in three words

**Simple. Honest. Human.**

## Words we use

- clear, plain language a non-technical founder can follow
- "you" and "we" — direct address, not third-person distance
- concrete verbs: build, ship, test, fix, launch
- "here's how" and "in practice" — show don't tell
- short sentences, especially when explaining technical concepts

## Words we avoid

- transform, revolutionise, disrupt, unlock, leverage, supercharge
- cutting-edge, next-generation, state-of-the-art
- "AI-powered" as a standalone selling point
- robotic, passive voice constructions ("it can be utilised to...")
- jargon without an immediate plain-English follow-up

## Claims we must never make

- "We use AI to transform your business."
- Any promise of guaranteed outcomes or revenue figures
- Comparisons that name competitors unless factually verified
- Anything that implies AI replaces human judgement entirely

## How we sign off

Warm and direct — like a knowledgeable friend wrapping up a call, not a vendor closing a deal.

**Examples:**
- "Let me know if anything needs unpacking."
- "Happy to dig into this with you."
- "Talk soon."

Avoid formal closings like "Best regards" or "Yours sincerely." Never use "Cheers" if the tone of the email has been serious.
